August 27th, 2026

My first few days of university have been an incredible experience, from events almost every hour, exploring around campus, meeting new people from various backgrounds, and attending my first classes have been an absolute blast. From what I have heard from everyone, this feeling of euphoria tends to only last a couple weeks before being replaced with miserable work. I believe though it's what you make of it that counts. My favorite part so far would probably be exploring all the "tech dumps" on campus which are boxes of discared technology that you can rumage through, and I've gotten really lucky. Besides university, adjusting to the American way of life has been surprisingly easy, though my blood sugar is through the roof. The things my mom used to say I couldn't have or eat turns out, to be not all that bad (within moderation). To summarize, I am just living in the moment.
